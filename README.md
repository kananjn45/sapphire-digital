About
Sapphire Digital Invites offers stylish, customizable digital invitations for weddings, parties, and events. Easily design, personalize, and share your invites online—perfect for modern celebrations seeking elegance, convenience, and eco-friendly solutions.

The site includes:
Static and video invitation galleries
Modern, responsive layout
Elegant theme with a signature pink header and fluid grid
Mobile-first design for usability on all devices
Easy navigation across all invite types

Features:
Static Invites Gallery:
Browse beautiful, themed invitation cards for every occasion.
Short Video Invites:
Instantly engaging event teasers and social-friendly invitations.
Long Video Invites:
Deeper stories for weddings, reunions, and detailed events.
Unified Navigation:
Left-aligned logo and brand in a standout pink block, with nav links and responsive hamburger toggle.
Testimonials & Contact:
Built-in testimonials and contact options for creators or feedback.

Tech Stack
HTML5
CSS3
Bootstrap 5 (CDN) for responsive navigation
